Demo Exports LLP — invoice payment

A cross-border client paying an Indian exporter. This payment flows straight into the AutoFIRC compliance pipeline.

Sandbox: log in with a personal sandbox account from the PayPal developer portal. After capture, the webhook drives classification → GST documents → bank petition → eBRC. Watch it in the dashboard or with autofirc watch.

Cross-border only: under PayPal's India policy, Indian merchants can accept international payments only — Indian-issued PayPal accounts cannot check out here. AutoFIRC enforces the same rule downstream: any payment from an Indian payer is rejected before compliance processing.
